Cadence

Running music that reacts to your body. In private development.

ROLE
Solo, idea to device
YEAR
2026
STACK
Swift · SwiftUI · iPhone · Apple Watch
STATUS
Private development

01 · WHAT IT DOES

Running playlists don't know how your run is going. A slow song lands just as you want to push, or a fast one arrives when you should be recovering.

Cadence is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that follows your body during a run and chooses the next song to match. The idea behind it: the music should lead the runner, not just follow them. It decides, moment by moment, whether you should push, hold or ease off, and picks music that pulls you that way.

02 · ON A RUN

  • Push, Hold or Ease. The app coaches through the music, not through a voice in your ear, and it changes course calmly rather than flipping every few seconds.
  • Watch first. The run lives on your wrist; the phone can stay in your pocket.
  • A haptic metronome on the Watch, and a short drill for anyone working on their step rate.
  • Real-world running. Stopping at a crossing doesn't kill the mood, and the music keeps coming through pauses.
  • Lock Screen controls to pause, push or ease without unlocking, plus a Home Screen widget with your last run.
  • After the run, a view of how each song landed, so the next run is picked better.

03 · HOW IT WAS BUILT

Built with AI-assisted development (Claude Code) since June 2026. I set the product idea and the behaviour, and tested every build on real runs with my own iPhone and Apple Watch. After each run I went through what happened with the model and decided the next batch of changes. The model wrote the Swift.

The hardest part wasn't the coaching, it was the platform: iOS killed the app three times for blocking the main thread while talking to the music player, which forced a rebuild of the whole playback layer.
