The operator

Most of what's on this site started as a queue that grew faster than the team. My default move is to find the part of the job that follows rules, write those rules down as code, and leave people the part that needs judgement.

I started out in procurement, where my first automations were spreadsheets that refused to stay manual. These days they're agents.

Off the clock I run (hence two of the cases above), ski, lift, and sing in musical theatre around Milan.
